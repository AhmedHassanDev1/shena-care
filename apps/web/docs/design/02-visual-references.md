# Shena Care — Pinterest Visual Research v0.1

**Status:** Research, not approved design.

## Brand Context

Shena Care is a personal care companion that guides users toward suitable skincare and haircare products, helps them build routines, and enables direct purchase.

Visual direction: Warm & Modern.

Final colors, typography, imagery style, and graphic motif are not yet approved.

## Pinterest Reference Set

| Reference | What to Study | What to Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| [AMAYA — Warm Light](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/1085086103983014895/) | Lighting, materials, restrained warmth | Copying packaging or luxury cues |
| [Skincare Editorial](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/155866837097921140/) | Editorial rhythm and typography | Organic-only brand messaging |
| [Product Flat Lay](https://dk.pinterest.com/pin/482307441338544972/) | Shadow, background texture, product composition | Unrealistic replacement of actual product packaging |
| [Arabic Cosmetic Branding](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/870179959233249579/) | Arabic brand typography | Decorative fonts that impair reading |
| [Four-Step Routine](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/813251645231723248/) | Clear visual sequence | Overcrowded infographics |
| [Wayfinding Identity](https://in.pinterest.com/pin/340303315605827223/) | Visual guidance and continuity | Decorative arrows with no meaning |

## Proposed Art Direction

1. Warm editorial foundations.
2. Distinctive graphic motif inspired by guidance and continuity.
3. Arabic-first typographic character.
4. Clean product presentation with trustworthy content.
5. Flexible semantic color tokens; temporary Olive theme only.

## Claude Code Instructions

Before creating any UI:

1. Open and inspect the original Pinterest references.
2. Identify concrete visual decisions, not generic style adjectives.
3. Propose two genuinely different visual-system directions.
4. Explain how each direction represents a personal care companion.
5. Describe what each direction deliberately avoids.
6. Preserve the existing frontend architecture and business logic.
7. Do not approve colors, fonts, logos, or components without review.

## Review Gates
