# Shena Care — Brand Direction v1

**Status:** Approved strategic direction; visual identity still exploratory.

## Brand Definition
Shena Care is a personal care companion that helps people understand their care needs, discover suitable products, build practical routines, and purchase products directly.

## Positioning
Guidance-led personal care commerce.

## Brand Promise
نساعدك تختار عنايتك بثقة.

*Working expression — not a finalized tagline.*

## Brand Personality
- Warm, not childish
- Intelligent, not robotic
- Trustworthy, not clinical
- Modern, not cold
- Helpful, not pushy
- Refined, not unnecessarily luxurious

## Experience Principles
1. Guidance is the differentiator; shopping remains easy.
2. Personalization must be explainable.
3. Users can browse and purchase without using AI.
4. Routines should feel achievable, not complicated.
5. Product claims must be grounded in verified information.
6. The assistant supports decisions; it does not pressure purchases.

## Visual Direction
Warm & Modern.

Use a temporary, token-based color theme. Final brand colors are not approved.

Explore visual expressions of guidance, continuity, clarity, and progress without relying on generic AI imagery.

## Brand Anti-Patterns
- Generic beauty storefront
- Medical diagnosis platform
- Organic-only positioning
- Chatbot-first interface
- Excessive AI gradients and sparkles
- Unexplained recommendation scores
- Unnecessary luxury styling

## Initial Scope
Skincare and Haircare, with the ability to expand into additional personal care categories.

## Open Decisions
- Final color palette
- Typography system
- Logo and visual motif
- Photography direction
- Component visual language
- Final tagline
